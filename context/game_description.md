# terminal-chef
low graphics cooking game, stress!

=============================================
ROOMS:

1. SERVICE
2. PLATING
3. COUNTERS
4. STOVES
5. FOOD PREP
6. PANTRY
7. CLEANING

Room layout:
[1]
[2][5][6]
[3][4][7]

[SERVICE]
[PLATING][PREP ROOM][PANTRY]
[COUNTERS][STOVES][CLEANING]
===============================================
INPUTS:
MOVE BETWEEN ROOMS: ARROW KEYS OR WASD
MENU SELECTIONS: 1-4

PULL UP "EVERYTHING": HOLD E, ON RELEASE, "ENTERED: ROOM X" MENU WILL PRINT AGAIN

PULL UP "RECIPES": HOLD R, ON RELEASE, "ENTERED: ROOM X" MENU WILL PRINT AGAIN

MOVEMENT KEYS CAN ONLY BE USED ON "ENTERED: ROOM X" MENUS
================================================
PROCESSES:

COOKING:
BEEF - 25 SECONDS, +1 TRASH UPON COMPLETION
CHICKEN - 20 SECONDS, +1 TRASH UPON COMPLETION
TOMATO - 15 SECONDS, +1 TRASH UPON COMPLETION
POTATO - 15 SECONDS, +1 TRASH UPON COMPLETION

BURNING - 15 SECONDS, +2 TRASH UPON COMPLETION
EATING - 45 SECONDS

DISH WASHER - 30 SECONDS, +1 TRASH UPON COMPLETION
TRASH DISPOSAL - 15 SECONDS

COUNTERS:
EXPIRE - 30 SECONDS, +3 TRASH UPON COMPLETION

CUTTING - 10 SECONDS, +1 TRASH UPON COMPLETION
WASHING - 5 SECONDS, +1 TRASH UPON COMPLETION
================================================
FOOD ITEMS:

INGREDIENTS:
IN FRIDGE: BEEF, CHICKEN, LETTUCE
IN PANTRY: TOMATO, POTATO, BREAD

INGREDIENT LABELS:
[RTP] - READY TO PLATE
[UNCUT] - NEEDS TO BE CUT IN PREP ROOM
[UNCOOKED] - NEEDS TO BE COOKED IN STOVES
[UNWASHED] - NEEDS TO BE WASHED IN PREP ROOM

PLATE LABELS:
[DIRTY] - NEEDS TO BE CLEANED IN DISH WASHER
[CLEAN]

INGREDIENTS START WITH:
BEEF - [UNCOOKED] [UNCUT]  Beef
    COOKED IN PAN -> [UNCUT] [RTP] Beef (Patty for burgers)
    CUT ON CUTTING BOARD -> [RTP] Beef (Steak for steak / steak salad)

CHICKEN - [UNWASHED] [UNCOOKED] [UNCUT] Chicken
    WASHED IN SINK -> [UNCOOKED] [UNCUT] Chicken
    COOKED IN PAN -> [UNCUT] [RTP] Chicken (For chicken sandwiches)
    CUT ON CUTTING BOARD -> [RTP] Chicken (For chicken and chicken / salad)

LETTUCE - [UNWASHED] [UNCUT] Lettuce
    WASHED IN SINK -> [UNCUT] Lettuce
    CUT ON CUTTING BOARD -> [RTP] Lettuce

TOMATO - [UNWASHED] [UNCUT] [UNCOOKED] Tomato
    WASHED IN SINK -> [UNCUT] [UNCOOKED] Tomato
    CUT ON CUTTING BOARD -> [UNCOOKED] [RTP] Tomato (For salads, burgers)
    COOKED IN POT -> [RTP] Tomato (For tomato soup)

POTATO - [UNCUT] [UNCOOKED] Potato
    CUT ON CUTTING BOARD -> [UNCOOKED] Potato
    COOKED IN POT -> [RTP] Potato

BREAD - [UNCUT] [RTP] Bread (for soups)
    CUT ON CUTTING BOARD -> [RTP] Bread


THE LEFT TO RIGHT ORDER IS THE ORDER THEY NEED TO BE ADDRESSED

ALL RECIPES FOODS:
WHEN CONSECUTIVE STEPS HAVE THE SAME NUMBER, THEIR ORDER IS INTERCHANGEABLE

===================================================================
TRASH LEVEL: GLOBAL VARIABLE

SCALES FROM 0-10

INCREASES WHEN:
PAN/POT PROCESS FINISHES: +1
CUTTING BOARD/SINK PROCESS FINISHES: +1
DISH WASHER FINISHES: +1
TAKE PLATE FROM A CUSTOMER: +1

ANY BURNING PROCESS FINISHES: +2
COUNTER ITEM PROCESS EXPIRES: +3

ALL PROCESSES STARTED WHEN LEVEL 8: +5 SEC
ALL PROCESSES STARTED WHEN LEVEL 9: +10 SEC
ALL PROCESSES STARTED WHEN LEVEL 10: +15 SEC
===================================================================
OVERALL GAMEPLAY:
    12 RANDOM MENU ITEMS - ORDER AND CONTENTS DECIDED AT START OF GAME
    MUST BE AT LEAST 8 DISTINCT ITEMS
        WHERE A BURGER AND BURGER W/ LETTUCE ARE DISTINCT

    ANY TIME AN ORDER IS FULFILLED, THE NEXT ORDER IS IMMEDIATELY PLACED

    AFTER 12 ITEMS:
        GAME REPORT, SEE EXAMPLE
===================================================================
SERVICE ROOM GAMEPLAY:

CUSTOMER STATUSES:
    WAITING
    EATING: 1-45 SEC
    DONE

ENTER ROOM BY PRESSING MOVEMENT KEYS

ENTER: "ENTERED: SERVICE"
    INTERACT W/ CUSTOMER 1 - PRESS 1
    INTERACT W/ CUSTOMER 2 - PRESS 2
    INTERACT W/ CUSTOMER 3 - PRESS 3
        CAN ONLY INTERACT WITH WAITING/DONE CUSTOMERS, NOT EATING

AFTER SELECTING INTERACT W/ CUSTOMER X: "CUSTOMER X"
    IF WAITING: SERVE FOOD
        GIVE THEM INVENTORY SLOT 1 - PRESS 1
        GIVE THEM INVENTORY SLOT 2 - PRESS 2
        GIVE THEM INVENTORY SLOT 3 - PRESS 3
            MUST BE EXACT ITEM THEY REQUESTED

    IF DONE: TAKE PLATE
        MOVE PLATE TO INVENTORY SLOT 1 - PRESS 1
        MOVE PLATE TO INVENTORY SLOT 2 - PRESS 2
        MOVE PLATE TO INVENTORY SLOT 3 - PRESS 3
            MUST BE OPEN INVENTORY SLOT
    RETURN TO "ENTERED SERVICE" - PRESS 4

AFTER INTERACTING W/ CUSTOMER:
    IF SERVE FOOD
        PRINT SERVED CUSTOMER 2
    IF TAKE PLATE
        PRINT NEW ORDER
    RETURNED TO "ENTERED: SERVICE ROOM"

ROOM CAN BE EXITED BY VALID MOVEMENT KEYS ONLY WHILE IN "ENTERED: X"
===================================================================
PLATING ROOM GAMEPLAY:

ENTER: "ENTERED: PLATING"
    INTERACT W/ PLATE 1 - PRESS 1
    INTERACT W/ PLATE 2 - PRESS 2
    INTERACT W/ PLATE 3 - PRESS 3

AFTER SELECTING A COUNTER:
    IF THERE IS A PLATE: "PLATE #X"
        ADD TO PLATE - PRESS 1
        PICK UP PLATE - PRESS 2
        RETURN TO "ENTERED: PLATING" - PRESS 4
    IF THERE IS NO PLATE: "SLOT #X: ADD PLATE"
        ADD INVENTORY 1 ITEM - PRESS 1
        ADD INVENTORY 2 ITEM - PRESS 2
        ADD INVENTORY 3 ITEM - PRESS 3
            ITEM MUST BE A WASHED PLATE

AFTER SELECTING ADD TO PLATE: "PLATE #X: ADD INGREDIENT"
    ADD INVENTORY 1 ITEM - PRESS 1
    ADD INVENTORY 2 ITEM - PRESS 2
    ADD INVENTORY 3 ITEM - PRESS 3
        INGREDIENT MUST HAVE [RTP] TAG
        MUST BE VALID INGREDIENT COMBINATION
    GO BACK TO "PLATE #X" - PRESS 4

AFTER SELECTING PICK UP PLATE: "PLATE #X: MOVE TO INVENTORY"
    MOVE PLATE TO INVENTORY 1 - PRESS 1
    MOVE PLATE TO INVENTORY 2 - PRESS 2
    MOVE PLATE TO INVENTORY 3 - PRESS 3
        CAN ONLY BE MOVED TO EMPTY INVENTORY SLOT
    GO BACK TO "PLATE #X" - PRESS 4


AFTER ADDING TO / PICKING UP PLATE:
    RETURNED TO "ENTERED: PLATING ROOM"

AFTER ADDING PLATE TO EMPTY SLOT
    RETURNED TO "ENTERED: PLATING ROOM"

ROOM CAN BE EXITED BY VALID MOVEMENT KEYS ONLY WHILE IN "ENTERED: X"
==================================================================
COUNTERS GAMEPLAY:

ENTER ROOM BY PRESSING MOVEMENT KEYS

COUNTER STATUSES:
    EMPTY
    STORING 1-30 SECS

AFTER 30 SECONDS ON THE COUNTER, ANY FOOD ITEM WILL EXPIRE: THIS MANIFESTS BY IT JUST DISAPPEARING. IF THERE WAS A PLATE WITH FOOD, IT BECOMES A [DIRTY] PLATE AFTER EXPIRATION. CAUSING +3 TRASH. A CLEAN PLATE CANNOT EXPIRE. SO IT WILL ALWAYS READ 30 SEC

ENTER: "ENTERED: COUNTERS"
    INTERACT W/ COUNTER 1 - PRESS 1
    INTERACT W/ COUNTER 2 - PRESS 2
    INTERACT W/ COUNTER 3 - PRESS 3

AFTER SELECTING COUNTER
    IF COUNTER IS EMPTY: "COUNTER X || STORE ITEM"
        ADD INVENTORY ITEM 1 TO COUNTER X - PRESS 1
        ADD INVENTORY ITEM 2 TO COUNTER X - PRESS 2
        ADD INVENTORY ITEM 3 TO COUNTER X - PRESS 3
    IF COUNTER HAS ITEM: "COUNTER X || TAKE ITEM"
        MOVE COUNTER X ITEM TO INVENTORY SLOT 1 - PRESS 1
        MOVE COUNTER X ITEM TO INVENTORY SLOT 2 - PRESS 2
        MOVE COUNTER X ITEM TO INVENTORY SLOT 3 - PRESS 3
    RETURN: PRESS 4

AFTER COMPLETING ACTION
    RETURNED TO "ENTERED: COUNTERS"

ROOM CAN BE EXITED BY VALID MOVEMENT KEYS ONLY WHILE IN "ENTERED: X"
===============================================================================
STOVES

PAN STATUSES:
    EMPTY, COOKING: 1-30 SEC [C], BURNING 1-15 SEC [B]
POT STATUSES:
    EMPTY, COOKING 1-30 SEC [C], BURNING 1-15 SEC [B]

AFTER ADDING AN ITEM TO A PAN/POT, THE COOKING PROCESS STARTS, IMMEDIATELY AFTER COOKING PROCESS ENDS, BURNING PROCESS BEGINS, AFTER BURNING ENDS, ITEM DISAPPEARS, PAN/POT BECOMES EMPTY, TRASH INCREASES +2, THE COOKING PROCESS ENDING SHOULD ADD +1 TO TRASH EVEN AS IT SWITCHES TO BURNING, SO THE CONSEQUENCE OF BURNING FOOD IS +3 TRASH OVERALL

STOVES GAMEPLAY: "ENTERED: STOVES"
    USE PAN 1 - PRESS 1
    USE PAN 2 - PRESS 2
    USE POT - PRESS 3

CANNOT SELECT PAN OR POT IF STATUS = COOKING

AFTER PRESSING PAN X / POT: "USING PAN #X / POT"
    IF EMPTY
        ADD INVENTORY 1 ITEM - PRESS 1
        ADD INVENTORY 2 ITEM - PRESS 2
        ADD INVENTORY 3 ITEM - PRESS 3
    IF BURNING
        MOVE TO INVENTORY 1 ITEM - PRESS 1
        MOVE TO INVENTORY 2 ITEM - PRESS 2
        MOVE TO INVENTORY 3 ITEM - PRESS 3

ROOM CAN BE EXITED BY VALID MOVEMENT KEYS ONLY WHILE IN "ENTERED: X"
=======================================================================
PREP ROOM

CUTTING BOARD STATUSES:
    EMPTY, CUTTING 1-15 SEC, COMPLETE
SINK STATUS:
    EMPTY, WASHING 1-15 SEC, COMPLETE

PREP GAMEPLAY: "ENTERED: PREP"
    USE CUTTING BOARD - PRESS 1
    USE SINK - PRESS 2
CANNOT SELECT CUTTING BOARD OR SINK IF STATUS = CUTTING / WASHING

AFTER PRESSING PAN X / POT: "PAN #X / POT"
    IF EMPTY
        ADD INVENTORY 1 ITEM - PRESS 1
        ADD INVENTORY 2 ITEM - PRESS 2
        ADD INVENTORY 3 ITEM - PRESS 3
    IF COMPLETE
        MOVE TO INVENTORY 1 - PRESS 1
        MOVE TO INVENTORY 2 - PRESS 2
        MOVE TO INVENTORY 3 - PRESS 3

ROOM CAN BE EXITED BY VALID MOVEMENT KEYS ONLY WHILE IN "ENTERED: X"

PANTRY GAMEPLAY:

ENTER: "ENTERED: PANTRY"
    INTERACT W/ FRIDGE - PRESS 1
    INTERACT W/ PANTRY - PRESS 2

AFTER SELECING FRIDGE: "IN THE FRIDGE"
    SELECT BEEF - PRESS 1
    SELECT CHICKEN - PRESS 2
    SELECT LETTUCE - PRESS 3
    GO BACK TO "ENTERED: PANTRY" - PRESS 4

AFTER SELECING SHELF: "ON THE SHELF"
    SELECT TOMATO - PRESS 1
    SELECT POTATO - PRESS 2
    SELECT LETTUCE - PRESS 3
    GO BACK TO "ENTERED: PANTRY" - PRESS 4

AFTER SELECTING ANY FOOD ITEM X: "SELECTED X"
    ADD TO INVENTORY 1 - PRESS 1
    ADD TO INVENTORY 2 - PRESS 2
    ADD TO INVENTORY 3 - PRESS 3
    GO BACK TO "IN THE FRIDGE" / "ON THE SHELF" - PRESS 4
        CAN ONLY BE ADDED TO OPEN INVENTORY SLOT

AFTER ADDING AN ITEM TO INVENTORY: RETURNED TO "ENTERED: PANTRY"

ROOM CAN BE EXITED BY VALID MOVEMENT KEYS ONLY WHILE IN "ENTERED: X"
====================================================================
CLEANING ROOM GAMEPLAY:

DISH WASHER STATUSES:
    EMPTY
    1 DIRTY, 2 DIRTY, 3 DIRTY
    1-30 SEC
    1 CLEAN, 2 CLEAN, 3 CLEAN

TRASH DISPOSAL STATUSES:
    EMPTY
    1-30 SEC

ENTER: "ENTERED: CLEANING"
    USE DISH WASHER - PRESS 1
    START TRASH DISPOSAL - PRESS 2
    USE DISCARD BIN - PRESS 3

        CANNOT SELECT DISH WASHER IF ITS ON (STATUS: 1-30 SEC)
        CANNOT SELECT TRASH DISPOSAL IF ITS ON (STATUS: 1-30 SEC)

AFTER SELECTING DISH WASHER: "DISH WASHER"
    IF EMPTY OR DIRTY:
        ADD PLATE - PRESS 1
        START DISH WASHER - PRESS 2
        GO BACK TO "ENTERED: CLEANING" - PRESS 4

    IF CLEAN:
        MOVE TO INVENTORY 1 - PRESS 1
        MOVE TO INVENTORY 2 - PRESS 2
        MOVE TO INVENTORY 3 - PRESS 3
        GO BACK TO "ENTERED: CLEANING" - PRESS 4
    IF THERE ARE MULTIPLE CLEAN PLATES, THE FIRST ONE ADDED WILL BE THE FIRST ONE MOVED TO THE INVENTORY
    INVENTORY SLOT MUST BE CLEAR

AFTER SELECTING ADD PLATE
    ADD INVENTORY 1 ITEM - PRESS 1
    ADD INVENTORY 2 ITEM - PRESS 2
    ADD INVENTORY 3 ITEM - PRESS 3
    GO BACK TO "DISH WASHER" - PRESS 4
ITEM MUST BE A PLATE, A PLATE WITH FOOD ON IT WILL WORK BUT THE FOOD WILL BE DISCARDED

AFTER SELECTING START DISH WASHER
    PRINTS STARTED DISH WASHER
    RETURNS TO "ENTERED: CLEANING"

AFTER SELECTING TRASH DISPOSAL
    USER WILL BE PROMPTED WITH SINGLE DIGIT X SINGLE DIGIT MULTIPLICATIONS UNTIL THEY GET THREE CORRECT, AFTER WHICH:
    PRINTS STARTED TRASH DISPOSAL
    TRASH DISPOSAL PROCESS IS STARTED
    RETURNS TO "ENTERED: CLEANING"

AFTER SELECTING DISCARD BIN
    DISCARD INVENTORY 1 ITEM - PRESS 1
    DISCARD INVENTORY 2 ITEM - PRESS 2
    DISCARD INVENTORY 3 ITEM - PRESS 3
    RETURN - PRESS 4

ROOM CAN BE EXITED BY VALID MOVEMENT KEYS ONLY WHILE IN "ENTERED: X"
